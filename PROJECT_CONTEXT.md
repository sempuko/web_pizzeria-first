# PROJECT CONTEXT — LA STELLA D'ORO

## Project

This project is a visual website concept for La Stella d'Oro, an independent Italian restaurant and pizzeria located at:

3 Rue Jacques Stella
69002 Lyon, France

The purpose of this first prototype is NOT to build the complete production website.

The first objective is to create a highly polished, visually distinctive interactive website concept that can be presented directly to the restaurant owner.

The prototype should immediately demonstrate what La Stella d'Oro could become online.

It should feel custom-designed for this specific restaurant, not like a restaurant template.

---

# THE RESTAURANT

La Stella d'Oro is a long-established independent Italian restaurant in central Lyon, located just next to Place de la République.

It is a small traditional restaurant rather than a trendy restaurant chain.

Its identity is built around:

- traditional Italian cuisine
- pizza and pasta
- generous portions
- affordable / reasonable prices
- warm personal service
- a small, intimate restaurant atmosphere
- regular customers
- long-term customer loyalty
- authenticity rather than trendiness

Popular dishes mentioned online include:

- pizza
- calzone
- pasta
- carbonara
- lasagne
- gorgonzola pasta
- tiramisu
- Italian desserts

The restaurant also offers takeaway and reservations.

---

# THE HUMAN STORY

One of the strongest assets of the restaurant is its owner / host, Sophie.

Customer reviews repeatedly describe her as:

- warm
- welcoming
- smiling
- attentive
- generous
- memorable

Customers often describe the restaurant with expressions similar to:

"comme à la maison"

Some customers have returned after many years and specifically mention that the restaurant and its hospitality remain memorable.

This is extremely important.

La Stella d'Oro should NOT be positioned as another generic modern pizza restaurant.

Its strongest differentiation is:

AUTHENTICITY + HUMANITY + HISTORY + GENEROSITY.

The website should communicate the feeling of discovering a small restaurant in Lyon that people return to for years.

---

# BRAND POSITIONING

The website should reinterpret the existing restaurant rather than reinvent it.

Desired positioning:

"Une adresse italienne lyonnaise que l'on découvre une fois et où l'on revient."

The experience should feel:

warm
authentic
confident
editorial
timeless
slightly nostalgic
European
personal
alive

But never:

rustic cliché
touristic
cheap
generic
corporate
luxury fine dining
startup-like

---

# CREATIVE DIRECTION

Visual concept:

OLD-SCHOOL ITALIAN TRATTORIA
×
CONTEMPORARY EUROPEAN EDITORIAL DESIGN

Imagine a traditional Lyon restaurant interpreted through the visual language of a contemporary Italian or French design studio.

The result should preserve the warmth and history of the restaurant while dramatically improving its digital image.

---

# VISUAL LANGUAGE

Use:

- strong editorial typography
- expressive type hierarchy
- large photography
- intentional asymmetry
- generous whitespace
- sophisticated grid
- controlled contrast
- subtle Italian references
- tactile visual details
- occasional handwritten or imperfect graphic elements
- elegant use of restaurant photography
- cinematic cropping
- confident typography

The design may borrow inspiration from:

Italian editorial design
independent magazines
European hospitality branding
classic trattoria graphics
contemporary food photography
fashion editorial layouts

Do not copy an existing restaurant website.

---

# COLORS

Avoid automatically using the Italian flag as the color system.

Possible palette direction:

Warm ivory / paper
Deep tomato red
Dark wine / burgundy
Near black
Muted olive
small touches of warm metallic / gold

Colors should feel slightly aged and tactile rather than digitally saturated.

---

# TYPOGRAPHY

Typography should be one of the main visual elements of the website.

Potential direction:

A characterful editorial serif
+
a clean contemporary sans serif.

Typography can occasionally become oversized and architectural.

Avoid generic startup typography.

Avoid making everything centered.

Avoid excessive uppercase text.

---

# PHOTOGRAPHY

Food photography should feel real, generous and tactile.

Prefer:

- close-ups
- imperfect compositions
- hands / serving
- pizza directly on the table
- pasta texture
- wine glasses
- restaurant interior
- details of the room
- tables and red-checkered elements if appropriate
- warm evening atmosphere

Avoid:

- generic stock pizza imagery
- floating ingredients
- artificial AI-food aesthetics
- hyper-perfect commercial food photography

Whenever suitable, real restaurant photography should be prioritized.

---

# DESIGN PRINCIPLES

The site must NOT resemble:

- a generic restaurant template
- a SaaS landing page
- a food delivery application
- a luxury Michelin restaurant
- an Instagram-inspired trendy pizza startup

Avoid:

- excessive rounded cards
- excessive pills
- glassmorphism
- generic gradients
- blobs
- excessive shadows
- decorative 3D elements
- generic AI imagery
- unnecessary cards
- generic icon grids

Prefer composition over containers.

Prefer typography over UI decoration.

Prefer storytelling over feature lists.

---

# EXPERIENCE

The website should feel calm at first, then reveal personality through motion.

Motion should be:

subtle
intentional
premium
tactile

Possible interactions:

- editorial image reveals
- subtle clipping / mask transitions
- text appearing with controlled stagger
- image scale on scroll
- restrained parallax
- menu underline interactions
- typography transitions
- slight movement between image and text layers

Never animate everything.

Motion must reinforce the atmosphere.

---

# FIRST PROTOTYPE

For the initial concept, build approximately 2–3 desktop viewports.

Do NOT build the full website yet.

The prototype must be strong enough that the restaurant owner can immediately imagine the finished website.

Suggested structure:

## VIEWPORT 01 — HERO

Navigation

La Stella d'Oro identity

One extremely strong restaurant / food image

Short positioning line

Possible copy direction:

"Une adresse italienne au cœur de Lyon."

or

"À deux pas de la République.
Une table italienne où l'on revient."

Primary actions:

Découvrir la carte
Réserver

Address may appear subtly:

3 rue Jacques Stella — Lyon 2e

The hero should have a strong visual signature.

---

## VIEWPORT 02 — STORY

Introduce the personality of the restaurant.

Possible editorial statement:

"Comme à la maison.
Depuis des années."

Explain briefly that La Stella d'Oro is a small Italian address in central Lyon known for its generous cuisine and warm welcome.

The layout should be visual rather than text-heavy.

Use photography and typography.

---

## VIEWPORT 03 — FOOD / EXPERIENCE

Feature several recognizable dishes.

Possible direction:

Pizza
Pasta
Tiramisu

Do not necessarily use traditional cards.

Explore editorial composition.

Include a strong call to action:

Voir la carte

or

Réserver une table

---

# COMMERCIAL OBJECTIVE

Remember that the prototype has two audiences.

AUDIENCE 1:
The restaurant owner.

The owner must think:

"This looks much more professional than our current online presence."

"This actually looks like our restaurant."

"This could help people discover us."

"I would like to have this website."

AUDIENCE 2:
Future restaurant customers.

They must think:

"This place has character."

"I want to eat there."

"I want to see the menu."

"I want to reserve."

---

# IMPORTANT

Do not immediately generate code when asked to explore a new direction.

First think as an art director.

Define the visual idea.

Then define the layout.

Then implement.

Existing restaurant character should always take priority over generic web-design trends.
